Phase A — AdamH LR & Warmup Sweep (10,000 steps)

Goal: Find the best AdamH hyperparameters (LR, warmup) at a fixed 10,000-step budget to establish L_target.
Result: The default config (lr=0.018, wu=250) is the winner. Higher LR (0.025) undershoots; lower LR (0.013) gives nearly identical performance; no warmup (wu=0) hurts significantly; longer warmup (wu=1000) gives negligible improvement.

Phase B — MuonH Step Minimisation

Goal: Find the fewest MuonH training steps to match L_target = 3.18194, using LR-budget scaling (lr_new = 0.018 × 10000 / T) to maintain an equivalent total LR integral.
Result: MuonH at 8,000 steps (lr=0.022) achieves val_loss=3.18021, beating L_target with a 20% step reduction. At 6,500 steps, budget-equivalent LR is insufficient and even boosting LR overshoots.
